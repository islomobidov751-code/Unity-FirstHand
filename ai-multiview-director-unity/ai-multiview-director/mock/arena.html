What this page does

It plays the part of the Unity build: a scripted session of the chosen game. Each camera is published to MediaMTX at /camN/whip, and telemetry goes to 20 times a second.

Keep this window visible while testing: browsers slow down hidden tabs, which freezes the session. Close it before connecting the real game, so only one game is connected.

The red border is the camera the dashboard has on air. Dialogue lines here are placeholders written for the mock.
